Piss Poor Idea venue placement agreement (version 2026-10-v1)

This agreement is between Piss Poor Idea ("we", "us") and the venue named in this signup ("you"). By signing, you confirm you're authorized to make this agreement for the venue.

  1. Permission to place stickers. You let us provide QR code stickers for your restrooms, which you put up yourself in spots you choose. You can move or remove them at any time.
  2. No cost. You pay nothing: no setup fee, no equipment, no monthly charge.
  3. What guests see. Scanning a sticker opens a page with your venue's name and social links, one ad, and buttons to rate the restroom or report a problem. Ads follow our published advertising policy. Political ads appear only if you opt in, and you can change that at any time.
  4. Revenue share. Each month we pool 90% of the ad revenue across all participating venues and split it by each venue's share of unique scans, as described on our site. We keep 10%. Payouts are made monthly once your balance reaches $25; smaller balances roll over. Earnings depend on scans and ad sales, and we don't guarantee any amount.
  5. Tax information. Before we pay you, we'll ask for a completed IRS Form W-9. We may report payments as required by law.
  6. Ratings and reports. Guest ratings, comments and maintenance reports for your restrooms are shown to you on your private board. Your venue appears on our public leaderboard only if you opt in.
  7. Data. We handle guest data as described in our privacy policy. We don't sell it.
  8. Ending this agreement. Either of us can end this agreement at any time with notice by email. When it ends, please take down the stickers; any balance owed to you is paid in the next payout cycle.
  9. Changes. If we change these terms, we'll email you at least 30 days before the change takes effect. You can end the agreement instead of accepting the change.
  10. Responsibility. You're responsible for your premises, and we're responsible for the ads and pages we serve. Neither of us is liable to the other for indirect or consequential losses.
