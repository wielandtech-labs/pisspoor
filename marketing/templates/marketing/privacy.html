Privacy

You scanned a QR code in a bathroom. Here's exactly what we do with that.

What we record

  • That a sticker was scanned, and when. This is how venues get paid.
  • A one-way daily fingerprint. A keyed hash of your IP address, browser user-agent, the date and a random one-day cookie, so one person scanning twice in a day counts once. We never store the IP address or the cookie itself, and because the date is part of the hash, it can't link your visits across days.
  • Anything you choose to submit: a star rating, a maintenance report, an optional comment. Comments are shown to the venue.
  • Sponsor clicks, counted the same way.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no names, no emails (unless you fill out our business contact form).
  • No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, no analytics scripts on these pages. Our only cookies are a security token that protects the forms and the random one-day counting cookie above; it expires after 24 hours and means nothing outside this site.
  • No camera, location or contacts access. It's a web page.
  • We don't sell data. Advertisers see totals, not people.

Sponsor links

When you tap a sponsor's button, you leave our site. Their privacy policy applies from there.

Questions? Use the contact form on the homepage.
