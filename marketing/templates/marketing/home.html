The money

90% to the venues. 10% to us. Split by traffic.

Advertisers buy the whole network. Each month we total the ad revenue, keep 10%, and divide the rest among venues by unique scans: one person, one sticker, one day. Bots and link previews don't count, and neither does the bartender scanning 400 times.
