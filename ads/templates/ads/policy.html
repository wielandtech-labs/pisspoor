Advertising policy

Our ads run in bathrooms: a captive audience, sometimes including kids, next to a venue's name. So every ad is reviewed by a person before it runs, and the rules are strict.

What we don't

  • Illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia, and anything promoting drug use
    Not on our walls. Not in our stalls.
  • Sexual services, escorts, solicitation, or adult content
    It's a bathroom, not a booth.
  • Weapons, ammunition, and accessories
    Nothing that goes bang. Not even in the parking lot.
  • Alcohol, cannabis, tobacco and vaping, and gambling or sports betting
    We have standards, you know. These are below us. (For now.)
  • Hate, harassment, or content targeting people for who they are
  • Scams, get-rich-quick schemes, crypto investment pitches, and predatory lending
  • Medical, health or financial claims you can't back up
  • Anything deceptive, including ads dressed up as something they're not

How review works

  • Every campaign starts as pending and runs only after approval.
  • Approval covers the exact link we reviewed. Change the destination and the ad stops until it's reviewed again.
  • We can reject or pull any ad, and every ad is labeled Sponsored.

Political ads

We put politicians where they belong.

  • Political ads must include a "paid for by" disclaimer, which we show word for word. You're responsible for its wording meeting the rules for your race.
  • Political ads are labeled Political ad, and ads made in whole or substantially with AI carry that disclosure.
  • They run only at venues that have opted in to political ads.
  • Every approved political ad is listed in our public political ad archive: who paid, when it ran, and how many times it was shown.

Questions about a campaign? Use the contact form on the homepage.
